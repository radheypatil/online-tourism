Andhra Pradesh – The Sunrise State

From serene beaches to spiritual temples, from lush valleys to ancient heritage – Andhra has it all.

Introduction

Andhra Pradesh, located on the southeastern coast of India, is a diverse land of spirituality, nature, and culture. Known as the "Sunrise State," it is home to beautiful coastlines, vibrant festivals, and mouthwatering Andhra cuisine like spicy curries and biryanis. Whether it’s the beaches of Visakhapatnam, the sacred Tirupati temple, or the lush Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh offers something unforgettable for every traveler.

Top Cities & Destinations

Visakhapatnam

A coastal gem famous for beaches, Kailasagiri Hills, and the INS Kursura submarine museum.
Vijayawada

Known as the business capital of Andhra Pradesh, with attractions like Kanaka Durga Temple and Prakasam Barrage.
Tirupati

Home to the world-famous Sri Venkateswara Temple, a major pilgrimage destination.

Culture & Food

Kuchipudi Dance

The classical dance form of Andhra Pradesh, blending storytelling and graceful movements.

Spicy Andhra Cuisine

Known for fiery flavors — try Gongura Pachadi, spicy curries, and coastal seafood delights.

Vibrant Festivals

Festivals like Sankranti and Ugadi bring the state alive with traditions, kites, and feasts.
