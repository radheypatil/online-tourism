Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu

Experience Portuguese heritage, golden beaches, and tribal culture in this coastal paradise.

Introduction

This Union Territory is a blend of sun-kissed beaches, forts, churches, wildlife, and tribal heritage. With Daman and Diu on the west coast showcasing Portuguese influence and Silvassa nestled in greenery, it is a unique mix of nature, culture, and history.

Top Attractions

Daman – Devka Beach

Golden sands and a lively beachside perfect for relaxation.

Diu Fort

A majestic Portuguese fort offering panoramic sea views.

Silvassa – Vasona Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to lions and a rich variety of flora & fauna.

Local Experiences

Portuguese Heritage

Discover colonial churches, forts, and architecture in Diu & Daman.

Tribal Culture

Explore tribal art, dance, and traditions in Silvassa.

Local Cuisine

Taste fresh seafood, Portuguese-inspired dishes, and tribal delicacies.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for beach weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Diu Airport, Surat Airport (near Daman); By Train – Vapi (near Daman), Veraval (near Diu); By Road – Well connected via Gujarat & Maharashtra highways.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cabs, and rental bikes are available.
