Udaipur – The City of Lakes

Experience the romance of palaces, shimmering lakes, and royal culture.

Introduction

Udaipur, often called the "Venice of the East," is renowned for its stunning lakes, grand palaces, and regal charm. Surrounded by the Aravalli Hills, Udaipur is a city of timeless beauty where royalty meets romance. From Lake Pichola to the magnificent City Palace, it’s a destination loved by travelers worldwide.

Top Attractions

City Palace

An architectural masterpiece overlooking Lake Pichola, showcasing Rajput grandeur.

Lake Pichola

A picturesque lake surrounded by ghats, temples, and the iconic Lake Palace.

Fateh Sagar Lake

A scenic artificial lake perfect for boating and sunset views.

Local Experiences

Shopping in Bazaars

Explore traditional bazaars filled with handicrafts, paintings, and jewelry.

Authentic Cuisine

Enjoy Dal Baati Churma, Gatte ki Sabzi, and other Rajasthani delicacies.

Festivals of Udaipur

Witness colorful festivals like Gangaur and Mewar Festival celebrated with pomp.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: September to March for pleasant weather and festivities.

How to Reach: By Air - Maharana Pratap Airport; By Train - Udaipur City Railway Station; By Road - Well connected via NH27 and NH76.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and boats on the lakes.
