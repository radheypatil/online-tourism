Nagaland – The Land of Festivals

Experience tribal traditions, rolling valleys, vibrant festivals, and breathtaking landscapes.

Introduction

Nagaland, located in Northeast India, is known for its diverse tribal culture, picturesque landscapes, and colorful festivals. With mist-covered hills, warm people, and traditions deeply rooted in heritage, Nagaland is truly a unique cultural destination.

Top Attractions

Kohima

The capital city, famous for the Kohima War Cemetery and the vibrant Hornbill Festival.

Dimapur

The commercial hub of Nagaland, with ancient ruins and easy access to the state.

Dzükou Valley

A breathtaking valley with rolling green hills and seasonal wildflowers.

Mokokchung

Known as the cultural hub of the Ao Naga tribe, rich in traditions and heritage.

Culture & Food

Hornbill Festival

Known as the “Festival of Festivals,” showcasing Naga traditions, dance, music, and cuisine.

Naga Cuisine

Famous for smoked pork, bamboo shoot dishes, and unique local flavors.

Tribal Heritage

Explore the diverse traditions, attire, and crafts of Nagaland’s 16 major tribes.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to March, especially during the Hornbill Festival.

How to Reach: By Air – Dimapur Airport; By Train – Dimapur Railway Station; By Road – National highways connect Nagaland with Assam and Manipur.

Local Transport: Shared taxis, buses, and cabs are widely available across towns and villages.
