Manipur – The Jeweled Land of the North-East

A serene state of scenic landscapes, rich traditions, vibrant dances, and floating wonders.

Introduction

Known as the “Jeweled Land,” Manipur is one of the most picturesque states in India. With its beautiful Loktak Lake, ancient culture, rich festivals, and welcoming people, Manipur is a hidden gem of the North-East. Its capital Imphal blends history with modern charm, while villages like Andro showcase traditional lifestyle and crafts.

Top Attractions

Imphal

The capital city, rich in history, famous for Kangla Fort, Ima Keithel (women’s market), and vibrant culture.

Loktak Lake

The largest freshwater lake in North-East India, known for its unique floating phumdis and Keibul Lamjao National Park.

Andro

A quaint village near Imphal, famous for pottery, traditional houses, and rich cultural heritage.

Culture & Food

Manipuri Dance

One of the classical dances of India, Manipuri Raslila is celebrated for its graceful movements and devotion to Lord Krishna.

Manipuri Cuisine

Healthy and organic meals featuring dishes like Eromba (vegetable stew with fish) and Chamthong (vegetable soup).

Sangai Festival

A grand annual festival showcasing Manipuri dance, food, sports, and cultural richness.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air – Imphal Airport connects to major Indian cities; By Train – Nearest railhead is Dimapur; By Road – Well-connected with Guwahati and other NE states.

Local Transport: Taxis, shared cabs, and auto-rickshaws are common in cities and towns.
