Thiruvananthapuram – Kerala’s Capital City

Home to the grand Padmanabhaswamy Temple, historic museums, and golden beaches.

Introduction

Thiruvananthapuram, also known as Trivandrum, is the capital city of Kerala. Blending traditional heritage with modern charm, it is famous for the Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, colonial architecture, and serene beaches like Kovalam. With its museums, palaces, and rich cultural festivals, the city is both a spiritual and cultural hub.

Top Attractions

Padmanabhaswamy Temple

A magnificent temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, known for its grandeur and wealth.

Napier Museum

An iconic museum showcasing Kerala’s rich cultural heritage, art, and artifacts.

Kuthiramalika Palace

A traditional Kerala palace with wooden carvings, built by Maharaja Swathi Thirunal.

Local Experiences

Kovalam Beach

Relax on one of Kerala’s most famous beaches, known for its lighthouse and golden sands.

Kerala Cuisine

Taste authentic Kerala dishes like Sadya, fish curry, and banana chips.

Onam Festival

Experience Kerala’s grand harvest festival with cultural programs and floral carpets.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air - Trivandrum International Airport; By Train - Thiruvananthapuram Central Railway Station; By Road - Well-connected to other cities.

Local Transport: Buses, taxis, and auto-rickshaws are widely available.
