Andaman & Nicobar Islands – Tropical Paradise

Pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, coral reefs, and rich history await in this island haven.

Introduction

The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are a union territory in the Bay of Bengal, offering some of the most beautiful beaches in India. Known for scuba diving, snorkeling, and vibrant marine life, they are also steeped in history, with landmarks like the Cellular Jail. With turquoise waters and white sands, it’s a dream getaway for nature and adventure lovers.

Top Attractions

Port Blair

Capital city known for Cellular Jail, museums, and cultural heritage.

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

Famous for Radhanagar Beach, diving spots, and eco-tourism.

Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)

Known for coral reefs, biodiversity, and tranquil beaches.

Ross Island

Historic ruins of British settlement amidst natural beauty.

Local Experiences

Scuba Diving

Dive into vibrant coral reefs and explore marine biodiversity.

Snorkeling

Enjoy snorkeling in crystal-clear lagoons with colorful fishes.

Sunset at Radhanagar Beach

Witness breathtaking sunsets on one of Asia’s best beaches.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to April (ideal for water sports and sightseeing).

How to Reach: By Air – Veer Savarkar International Airport (Port Blair); By Ship – Regular ships from Chennai, Kolkata, and Vizag.

Local Transport: Ferries, boats, auto-rickshaws, and taxis are commonly used.
