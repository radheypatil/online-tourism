Arunachal Pradesh – The Land of Dawn-Lit Mountains

Explore Tawang, Ziro, Itanagar, and Bomdila — a paradise of monasteries, valleys, and tribal culture.

Introduction

Arunachal Pradesh, located in the northeastern tip of India, is known for its breathtaking landscapes, Buddhist monasteries, and rich tribal traditions. The state is blessed with snow-clad peaks, serene valleys, and colorful festivals that celebrate its cultural diversity. From the sacred town of Tawang to the lush valleys of Ziro, Arunachal is a hidden gem waiting to be explored.

Top Attractions

Tawang

Home to the famous Tawang Monastery, stunning lakes, and snow-capped mountains.

Ziro Valley

UNESCO World Heritage site, known for Apatani culture and lush paddy fields.

Itanagar

The capital city, featuring Ita Fort, Ganga Lake, and rich tribal culture.

Bomdila

A serene hill town with a monastery, apple orchards, and panoramic Himalayan views.

Culture & Food

Festivals

Colorful festivals like Losar and Ziro Festival of Music celebrate the state’s vibrant tribal culture.

Food

Relish local dishes like bamboo shoot curry, smoked meat, and Apong (traditional rice beer).

Dance & Music

Traditional tribal dances and folk songs reflect the harmony between people and nature.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March to October for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Lilabari Airport (Assam) with connections to Arunachal; By Train - Naharlagun Railway Station; By Road - National Highways from Assam.

Local Transport: Shared jeeps, buses, and cabs are the main modes of travel.
