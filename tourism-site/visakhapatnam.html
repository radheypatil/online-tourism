Visakhapatnam – The Jewel of the East Coast

Sun-kissed beaches, emerald hills & cultural charm — plan your Vizag escape.

Introduction

Visakhapatnam, also known as Vizag, is Andhra Pradesh’s largest coastal city, famous for its pristine beaches, lush green hills, and rich cultural heritage. It is one of India’s oldest port cities and a thriving hub of tourism, trade, and naval operations. From the golden sands of Rushikonda to the panoramic views from Kailasagiri, Vizag offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure.

Top Attractions

Rushikonda Beach

A golden sandy beach popular for swimming, surfing, and breathtaking sunrises.

Kailasagiri Hill Park

A hilltop park with giant Shiva-Parvati statues and a ropeway offering stunning views of the coastline.

Submarine Museum

INS Kurusura, a decommissioned submarine, converted into a museum showcasing India’s naval glory.

Local Experiences

Savor Andhra Seafood

Indulge in mouth-watering seafood delicacies and the famous Bamboo Chicken from Vizag’s local eateries.

Shopping in Jagadamba Bazaar

Explore traditional handicrafts, Kondapalli toys, and Andhra Kalamkari fabrics.

Celebrate Local Festivals

Join in Sankranti Kite Festival and the vibrant Visakha Utsav to experience Vizag’s culture.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for cool weather and beach activities.

How to Reach: By Air – Visakhapatnam International Airport; By Train – Visakhapatnam Junction; By Road – Connected to major cities via NH16.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cabs, buses, and rental bikes are easily available.
