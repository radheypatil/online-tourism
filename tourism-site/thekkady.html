Thekkady – Kerala’s Wildlife & Spice Haven

Experience Periyar’s elephants, serene lakes, and lush spice plantations.

Introduction

Thekkady, nestled in Kerala’s Idukki district, is famous for the Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary and its vibrant spice plantations. A paradise for wildlife lovers and adventure seekers, Thekkady offers bamboo rafting, trekking, and the chance to see herds of elephants in their natural habitat. Its lush greenery and serene backwaters make it a must-visit destination for nature enthusiasts.

Top Attractions

Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

A world-renowned reserve where you can spot elephants, tigers, and exotic birds.

Spice Plantations

Tour aromatic plantations of cardamom, pepper, cinnamon, and cloves.

Periyar Lake

Enjoy boat cruises on the serene Periyar Lake amidst lush forest scenery.

Local Experiences

Bamboo Rafting

A thrilling adventure on bamboo rafts across Periyar Lake.

Trekking Trails

Hike through the lush forests of Periyar and spot wildlife on the way.

Spice Shopping

Take home Kerala’s world-famous spices fresh from the plantations.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: September to April for wildlife spotting and spice tours.

How to Reach: By Air - Madurai Airport (136 km); By Train - Kottayam (114 km); By Road - Well connected via NH183.

Local Transport: Jeeps, taxis, and guided tours are commonly available.
