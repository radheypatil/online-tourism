Odisha – Land of Temples and Beaches

Discover the timeless temples, golden beaches, vibrant arts, and natural wonders of Odisha.

Introduction

Odisha, located on the eastern coast of India, is renowned for its magnificent temples, pristine beaches, tribal culture, and traditional art forms. From the architectural marvel of Konark’s Sun Temple to the spiritual aura of Puri’s Jagannath Temple and the serenity of Chilika Lake, Odisha is a perfect mix of heritage and nature.

Top Attractions

Bhubaneswar

The ‘Temple City of India’ with over 700 ancient temples showcasing Odisha’s spiritual heritage.

Puri

Famous for the Jagannath Temple and its Rath Yatra, as well as beautiful golden beaches.

Konark

Home to the UNESCO World Heritage Sun Temple, an architectural and cultural wonder.

Cuttack

Known as the Silver City, it is famous for Barabati Fort and intricate silver filigree work.

Chilika Lake

Asia’s largest brackish water lagoon, a paradise for birdwatchers and nature lovers.

Raghurajpur

A heritage crafts village famous for Pattachitra paintings and Gotipua dance.

Culture & Food

Rath Yatra

The world-famous chariot festival of Lord Jagannath, held annually in Puri.

Odia Cuisine

Delight in Dalma, Pakhala Bhata, Rasagola, and seafood specialties unique to Odisha.

Pattachitra Art

Intricate traditional scroll paintings that depict mythological stories and folklore.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and major festivals.

How to Reach: By Air – Bhubaneswar Airport; By Train – Bhubaneswar, Puri, and Cuttack Stations; By Road – Well connected with major highways.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cycle rickshaws, cabs, and buses are widely available.
