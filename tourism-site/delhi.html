Delhi – The Heart of India

From Mughal-era wonders of Old Delhi to the modern vibrance of New Delhi, explore the capital city like never before.

Introduction

Delhi, India’s capital territory, is a tapestry of history, culture, and modern development. Old Delhi is famous for its Mughal architecture, bustling bazaars, and street food, while New Delhi showcases grand government buildings, wide avenues, and cosmopolitan lifestyles.

Top Attractions

India Gate (New Delhi)

A war memorial and iconic landmark symbolizing national pride.

Red Fort (Old Delhi)

A UNESCO World Heritage Site showcasing Mughal grandeur.

Lotus Temple

A stunning Baháʼí house of worship known for its flowerlike architecture.

Local Experiences

Chandni Chowk

A bustling bazaar in Old Delhi offering spices, textiles, and street food.

Street Food

Savor iconic dishes like paranthas, kebabs, and chaat.

Dilli Haat

A vibrant marketplace showcasing handicrafts and cuisines from across India.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Indira Gandhi International Airport; By Train – New Delhi, Old Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin Stations; By Road – Excellent highway connectivity.

Local Transport: Delhi Metro, buses, auto-rickshaws, app-based cabs.
