Chandigarh – The City Beautiful

A planned city blending modern architecture, green spaces, and rich Punjabi culture.

Introduction

Chandigarh, the joint capital of Punjab and Haryana, is one of the best-planned cities in India designed by Le Corbusier. Famous for its wide roads, clean environment, and gardens, it beautifully combines urban life with nature. The city is known for its Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, and vibrant Punjabi hospitality.

Top Attractions

Rock Garden

Unique sculptures made from recycled materials by Nek Chand.

Sukhna Lake

A serene lake perfect for boating, walking, and relaxing.

Zakir Hussain Rose Garden

Asia’s largest rose garden with thousands of varieties.

Capitol Complex

A UNESCO World Heritage Site showcasing modernist architecture.

Local Experiences

Punjabi Cuisine

Savor butter chicken, sarson da saag, and makki di roti with lassi.

Shopping & Nightlife

Trendy malls, vibrant nightlife, and bustling local markets.

Cultural Events

Attend cultural performances and exhibitions in this artistic city.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Chandigarh International Airport; By Train – Chandigarh Railway Station; By Road – Well connected with Delhi & Punjab cities.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cabs, and city buses are easily available.
