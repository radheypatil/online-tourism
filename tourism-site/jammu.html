Jammu & Kashmir – Paradise on Earth

Snow-capped mountains, serene valleys, holy shrines, and breathtaking landscapes await you.

Introduction

Jammu & Kashmir, often called "Paradise on Earth," is famous for its pristine natural beauty, Mughal gardens, spiritual destinations, and adventure tourism. From Srinagar’s Dal Lake to Vaishno Devi in Katra and the rugged landscapes of Leh-Ladakh, this UT offers diverse experiences for every traveler.

Top Attractions

Srinagar

The summer capital, known for Dal Lake, houseboats, and Mughal gardens.

Gulmarg

A skiing and adventure hub surrounded by snow-clad peaks.

Pahalgam

A picturesque valley, base for the Amarnath Yatra, known for rivers and pine forests.

Sonamarg

The “Meadow of Gold,” a gateway to Himalayan treks and glaciers.

Katra (Vaishno Devi)

One of the most visited Hindu pilgrimage sites dedicated to Mata Vaishno Devi.

Leh (Ladakh)

Famous for monasteries, Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, and unique culture.

Local Experiences

Kashmiri Cuisine

Relish Wazwan, Rogan Josh, Gushtaba, and saffron-infused Kahwa tea.

Pashmina Shopping

Shop exquisite Pashmina shawls, carpets, and handicrafts from Kashmir.

Shikara Ride

A relaxing ride on Dal Lake, offering views of floating gardens and markets.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March–October for sightseeing; December–February for snow activities.

How to Reach: By Air – Srinagar & Jammu Airports; By Train – Jammu Tawi Station; By Road – National highways from Delhi, Punjab, Himachal.

Local Transport: Taxis, buses, pony rides in hill areas, and Shikara boats in Srinagar.
