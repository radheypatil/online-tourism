West Bengal – Cultural Heart of India

From the colonial charm of Kolkata to the tea gardens of Darjeeling and the wild Sundarbans, Bengal is a blend of culture, nature, and heritage.

Introduction

West Bengal is a state of contrasts – from bustling Kolkata to the serene tea estates of Darjeeling, the mystical Sundarbans mangroves to the cultural heart of Shantiniketan. Known for art, literature, music, and delicious Bengali cuisine, West Bengal is a journey through India’s soul.

Top Attractions

Kolkata

City of Joy, home to Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, and cultural festivals.

Darjeeling

Famous for tea gardens, Himalayan views, and the toy train (UNESCO site).

Sundarbans

World’s largest mangrove forest, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.

Kalimpong

A quiet hill station with monasteries, flower markets, and Himalayan views.

Shantiniketan

Founded by Rabindranath Tagore, it’s the cultural capital with art, music, and heritage.

Digha

A popular beach destination on the Bay of Bengal.

Local Experiences

Durga Puja

The grandest festival of Bengal, celebrated with lights, art, music, and devotion.

Bengali Cuisine

Enjoy rasgulla, mishti doi, macher jhol (fish curry), and other delicacies.

Art & Handicrafts

Explore Bengal’s traditional handicrafts, kantha embroidery, and terracotta art.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March (pleasant weather, festivals like Durga Puja).

How to Reach: By Air – Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Airport (Kolkata); By Train – Sealdah, Howrah, New Jalpaiguri; By Road – Well-connected highways and buses.

Local Transport: Taxis, trams (Kolkata), metro, buses, and shared jeeps in hills.
