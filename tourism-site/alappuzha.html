Alleppey – The Venice of the East

Famous for its serene backwaters, houseboats, and lush paddy fields.

Introduction

Alappuzha, popularly known as Alleppey, is a paradise of tranquil backwaters, lush greenery, and houseboat cruises. Often called the ‘Venice of the East,’ it is famous for its canals, lagoons, and traditional Kerala houseboats. From breathtaking sunsets to delicious seafood, Alleppey offers a complete Kerala experience.

Top Attractions

Backwaters

A network of serene canals, lagoons, and lakes – best experienced on a houseboat cruise.

Nehru Trophy Boat Race

Witness Kerala’s thrilling snake boat race, held every year in August.

Alappuzha Beach

Golden sands, a historic pier, and mesmerizing sunsets await here.

Local Experiences

Snake Boat Race

Experience the cultural grandeur and excitement of Kerala’s traditional races.

Seafood Delicacies

Taste the authentic Kerala seafood, including Karimeen Pollichathu and prawn curry.

Coir Industry

Learn about Alleppey’s traditional coir-making industry and buy handicrafts.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to February for houseboat rides and pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air - Cochin International Airport (83 km); By Train - Alappuzha Railway Station; By Road - Well-connected by buses and cabs.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and ferries are commonly available.
