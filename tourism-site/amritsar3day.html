Discover Amritsar

Amritsar, the spiritual and cultural heart of Punjab, is home to the magnificent Golden Temple, bustling bazaars, and a rich history. Experience a blend of devotion, adventure, and local flavor over three unforgettable days.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Golden Temple & Old City

Morning: Visit the Golden Temple at sunrise, explore the serene Sarovar.
Afternoon: Explore the Old City, Jallianwala Bagh, and local markets.
Evening: Enjoy Langar at the Golden Temple and experience the spiritual ambiance.

Day 2: Wagah Border & Cultural Immersion

Morning: Relax at your hotel or local cafe.
Afternoon: Witness the Wagah Border Beating Retreat Ceremony.
Evening: Explore local culture, shops, and enjoy traditional Punjabi cuisine.

Day 3: Local Markets & Street Food

Morning: Visit Hall Bazaar and Katra Jaimal Singh Market.
Afternoon: Indulge in Amritsari kulchas, lassi, and street snacks.
Evening: Take a heritage walk and capture memorable photos before departure.

Travel Tips & Local Food

  • Best time to visit: October to March.
  • Respect local customs at religious sites.
  • Try Amritsari kulcha, chole bhature, and sweet lassi.
  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking in the Old City.
  • Carry sunscreen and stay hydrated.
