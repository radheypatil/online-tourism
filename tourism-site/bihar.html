Bihar – Land of Enlightenment

Journey through the land of Buddha, ancient universities, and vibrant culture.

Introduction

Bihar is one of India’s most culturally and historically significant states, known for its role in the life of Lord Buddha, the ancient Nalanda University, and the Mauryan Empire. The land blends spirituality, history, and a rich cultural identity.

Top Attractions

Bodh Gaya

A UNESCO World Heritage site where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree.

Patna

The capital city, rich in museums, riverfront ghats, and historical landmarks.

Nalanda

Ruins of the world’s oldest residential university, a center of learning in ancient India.

Rajgir

A sacred place associated with Buddha and Mahavira, featuring hot springs and peace pagoda.

Vaishali

One of the world’s first republics, important in both Buddhist and Jain traditions.

Culture & Food

Chhath Puja

Bihar’s grand festival dedicated to the Sun God, celebrated with devotion along river ghats.

Litti Chokha

The iconic dish of Bihar made with roasted wheat balls stuffed with spices, served with mashed veggies.

Madhubani Art

The world-famous folk painting style that narrates stories of mythology and nature.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Patna Airport; By Train – Patna & Gaya Junctions; By Road – Well-connected highways and buses.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and buses are widely available.
