Wayanad – Kerala’s Hill Paradise

Misty hills, forests, waterfalls, and unforgettable wildlife adventures.

Introduction

Wayanad, located in the Western Ghats of Kerala, is a land of misty mountains and lush forests. Known for its wildlife sanctuaries, trekking trails, waterfalls, and spice plantations, Wayanad is a paradise for nature and adventure lovers. From spotting elephants in the wild to hiking through scenic valleys, Wayanad offers an authentic slice of Kerala’s natural beauty.

Top Attractions

Edakkal Caves

Ancient rock carvings dating back thousands of years, offering history and scenic views.

Soochipara Waterfalls

A stunning three-tiered waterfall surrounded by dense forest, ideal for trekking.

Chembra Peak

A popular trekking spot offering breathtaking views and the heart-shaped Chembra Lake.

Local Experiences

Wildlife Safari

Spot elephants, deer, and exotic birds in Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary.

Trekking Adventures

Trek through tea plantations, spice gardens, and mist-covered valleys.

Kerala Cuisine

Taste authentic Kerala dishes including Malabar biryani and spicy curries.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to May for trekking and sightseeing.

How to Reach: By Air - Calicut International Airport (87 km away); By Train - Kozhikode Railway Station; By Road - Well connected via NH766.

Local Transport: Taxis, jeeps, and rental bikes are the best ways to explore.
