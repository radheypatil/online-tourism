Assam – The Land of Tea and Brahmaputra

Experience the beauty of Guwahati, Kaziranga, Majuli, Sivasagar, and Jorhat — where nature and heritage blend.

Introduction

Assam, nestled in the northeastern part of India, is famous for its sprawling tea gardens, the mighty Brahmaputra River, and the one-horned rhinoceros of Kaziranga. With its vibrant culture, traditional Bihu dance, and unique cuisine, Assam offers a beautiful mix of nature, history, and traditions.

Top Attractions

Guwahati

Home to the famous Kamakhya Temple and gateway to the Northeast.

Kaziranga National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for the one-horned rhinoceros and diverse wildlife.

Majuli

The world’s largest river island, known for its monasteries and cultural heritage.

Sivasagar

Historic town with Ahom-era monuments, temples, and tanks.

Jorhat

Known as the ‘Tea Capital of the World’, surrounded by lush tea estates.

Culture & Food

Bihu Festival

The most vibrant festival of Assam, celebrated with dance, music, and traditional feasts.

Cuisine

Assamese food is simple yet flavorful — from fish curry to rice with exotic herbs.

Tea Culture

World-renowned Assam tea is cultivated here, offering scenic views and tea tours.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to April for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (Guwahati); By Train - Guwahati, Jorhat, Dibrugarh; By Road - National Highways from major cities.

Local Transport: Buses, auto-rickshaws, shared cabs, and ferries (for Majuli).
