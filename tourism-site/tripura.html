Tripura – Land of Myths & Heritage

From the royal palaces of Agartala to the mystical rock carvings of Unakoti, Tripura is rich in history, spirituality, and natural beauty.

Introduction

Tripura, nestled in Northeast India, is a culturally vibrant state with deep historical roots. Known for its palaces, ancient temples, and scenic hills, the state offers a blend of tribal traditions, mythology, and natural wonders. It is one of India’s hidden gems waiting to be explored.

Top Attractions

Agartala

The capital city, famous for the Ujjayanta Palace and Neermahal, a lake palace known as the “Water Palace of the East.”

Unakoti

A unique archaeological site with thousands of ancient rock carvings dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Udaipur

Known for the Tripura Sundari Temple, one of the 51 Shakti Peethas, and picturesque lakes.

Culture & Food

Folk Dances

Tribal dances like Hojagiri and Garia showcase the vibrant traditions of Tripura.

Cuisine

Tripuri dishes like Mui Borok (fish stew) and Mosdeng (spicy chutney) highlight unique tribal flavors.

Handicrafts

Tripura is known for bamboo and cane handicrafts, showcasing the artistry of its tribal communities.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (Agartala); By Train – Agartala Railway Station; By Road – Connected to Assam and Bangladesh.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, buses, and taxis are available in major towns.
