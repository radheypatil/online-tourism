Mount Abu – The Hill Station of Rajasthan

Rajasthan’s only hill station, blessed with cool weather, lakes, and ancient temples.

Introduction

Mount Abu, nestled in the Aravalli Range, is Rajasthan’s only hill station. Known for its pleasant climate, serene lakes, and the magnificent Dilwara Temples, it is a favorite escape for tourists. The lush greenery, spiritual charm, and colonial history make it a unique gem in the desert state.

Top Attractions

Dilwara Temples

Exquisite marble temples built between the 11th and 13th centuries, famous for intricate carvings.

Nakki Lake

A picturesque lake surrounded by hills, perfect for boating and evening walks.

Guru Shikhar

The highest peak of the Aravalli Range, offering panoramic views of the surroundings.

Local Experiences

Sunset Point

A romantic spot to witness breathtaking sunsets over the Aravalli hills.

Mount Abu Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to leopards, sloth bears, and diverse flora and fauna.

Local Bazaars

Shop for handicrafts, Rajasthani textiles, and souvenirs at bustling markets.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

How to Reach: By Air - Udaipur Airport (185 km); By Train - Abu Road Station; By Road - Well-connected by buses and taxis.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and rented scooters.
