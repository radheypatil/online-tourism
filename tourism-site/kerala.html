Kerala – God's Own Country

Experience lush backwaters, serene beaches, tea gardens, and Ayurvedic traditions in Kerala.

Introduction

Kerala, often called "God's Own Country," is famous for its scenic landscapes, houseboats, Ayurvedic treatments, and cultural diversity. With tranquil backwaters, hill stations like Munnar, and pristine beaches like Kovalam, Kerala offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure.

Top Attractions

Munnar

A hill station with sprawling tea gardens, waterfalls, and cool climate.

Alappuzha (Alleppey)

The Venice of the East, famous for houseboat cruises on backwaters.

Kumarakom

A peaceful village by Vembanad Lake known for bird sanctuary and backwaters.

Thiruvananthapuram

Kerala’s capital city, home to Padmanabhaswamy Temple and museums.

Kovalam

A beach paradise with golden sands and water sports activities.

Wayanad

Known for its forests, wildlife, and trekking trails amidst misty hills.

Thekkady

Famous for Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary and spice plantations.

Culture & Food

Kathakali

A classical dance-drama known for elaborate costumes and storytelling.

Sadya

A traditional feast served on banana leaves with multiple vegetarian dishes.

Snake Boat Race

A spectacular boat race held during the Onam festival.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: September to March for pleasant weather; June to August for monsoon beauty.

How to Reach: By Air – Kochi, Trivandrum, and Calicut International Airports; By Train – Major cities well connected; By Road – Good highways and buses.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, ferries, and buses are widely available.
