Vijayawada – The Heart of Andhra Pradesh

Discover the charm, culture, and beauty of Vijayawada

Welcome to Vijayawada

Vijayawada is a vibrant city located on the banks of the Krishna River. Known for its rich cultural heritage, magnificent temples, and scenic landscapes, it offers travelers a perfect blend of history and modernity. With a warm climate and welcoming locals, Vijayawada is an ideal destination for sightseeing, food exploration, and experiencing festivals.

Top Attractions

Kanaka Durga Temple

A historic temple on Indrakeeladri Hill offering panoramic city views and spiritual ambiance.

Prakasam Barrage

A majestic structure over Krishna River, perfect for sunset views and photography.

Undavalli Caves

Ancient rock-cut caves featuring stunning sculptures and historical significance.

Bhavani Island

A beautiful island on Krishna River ideal for leisure, water sports, and nature walks.

Gandhi Hill

A peaceful hilltop park with a statue of Mahatma Gandhi and panoramic city views.

Bapu Museum

Museum showcasing rich art, artifacts, and cultural history of Andhra Pradesh.

Kondapalli Fort

Historic fort with panoramic views, famous for Kondapalli toys and heritage experience.

Local Experiences

Street Food & Cuisine

Taste the vibrant flavors of Andhra street food, from spicy snacks to sweet treats.

Festivals & Culture

Experience the grandeur of local festivals like Dasara and temple celebrations.

Shopping & Markets

Explore local markets and pick up Kondapalli toys, handicrafts, and souvenirs.

Travel Information

  • Best Time to Visit: October to March (pleasant climate)
  • By Air: Vijayawada Airport (VGA) connects to major Indian cities
  • By Train: Vijayawada Junction is well-connected with express trains
  • By Road: State buses, taxis, and private cabs are available
  • Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, city buses, and taxis
