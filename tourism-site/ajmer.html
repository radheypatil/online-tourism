Ajmer – The Spiritual Heart of Rajasthan

Famous for Ajmer Sharif Dargah, serene lakes, and cultural harmony.

Introduction

Ajmer is one of the most spiritually significant cities of Rajasthan, best known for the Ajmer Sharif Dargah, a major Sufi pilgrimage site visited by devotees from all faiths. Nestled amidst the Aravalli hills, the city also features beautiful lakes, forts, and cultural heritage. Its unique charm comes from the blend of spirituality and history.

Top Attractions

Ajmer Sharif Dargah

The sacred shrine of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, a revered Sufi saint.

Ana Sagar Lake

A scenic lake offering boat rides and peaceful sunset views.

Taragarh Fort

A historic fort with panoramic views of Ajmer city and the Aravalli hills.

Local Experiences

Pushkar Fair

A vibrant festival of camels, colors, and culture held nearby in Pushkar.

Street Food

Taste delicacies like Kadhi Kachori, Sohan Halwa, and authentic Rajasthani thali.

Local Bazaars

Shop for handicrafts, embroidered fabrics, and silver jewelry in bustling markets.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for cool weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Jaipur Airport (130 km); By Train - Ajmer Junction; By Road - Well-connected by NH48.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and cycle rickshaws.
