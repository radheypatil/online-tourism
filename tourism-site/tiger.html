Tiger Safari – Ranthambore

Venture into the wild heart of Rajasthan to track the majestic Bengal tiger in its natural habitat.

Why Ranthambore?

Ranthambore National Park, located in Rajasthan, is one of the best places in the world to spot the Royal Bengal Tiger. With its mix of dense forests, open grasslands, and ancient ruins, the park offers a thrilling blend of wildlife adventure and history. From elusive leopards to crocodiles basking in the sun, Ranthambore is a paradise for wildlife enthusiasts and photographers.

The Safari Experience

Spotting the Tiger

The thrill of seeing a Bengal tiger roaming free is unmatched and unforgettable.

Historic Ranthambore Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage site inside the park, blending history with wilderness.

Scenic Landscapes

Witness lakes, rocky cliffs, and lush forests that make Ranthambore breathtaking.

Travel Tips

  • Best Time: October to June (winters and summers offer good sightings).
  • Safari Zones: The park has 10 zones — tiger sightings are common in Zones 2, 3, and 4.
  • Safari Type: Choose between open Gypsy jeeps (6-seater) and Canters (20-seater).
  • Booking: Safaris must be booked online in advance due to limited slots.
