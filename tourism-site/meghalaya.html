Meghalaya – The Abode of Clouds

Land of misty hills, roaring waterfalls, sacred forests, and vibrant tribal culture.

Introduction

Meghalaya, meaning “Abode of Clouds,” is one of the most scenic states in India. With lush green landscapes, some of the world’s heaviest rainfall, living root bridges, and breathtaking valleys, Meghalaya is nature’s paradise. Its capital Shillong is often called the “Scotland of the East.”

Top Attractions

Shillong

The charming capital city, famous for Umiam Lake, Elephant Falls, Ward’s Lake, and vibrant nightlife.

Cherrapunji (Sohra)

Known for heavy rainfall, living root bridges, and Nohkalikai Falls – the tallest plunge waterfall in India.

Mawlynnong

Dubbed the “Cleanest Village in Asia,” it offers bamboo walkways, root bridges, and serene nature.

Dawki

Home to the crystal-clear Umngot River, where boats seem to float in the air.

Jowai

Famous for lakes, waterfalls, and rich Pnar tribal culture, making it a hidden gem of Meghalaya.

Culture & Food

Khasi Dance

Traditional Khasi dances, performed during festivals, celebrate nature and community life.

Meghalayan Cuisine

Popular dishes include Jadoh (rice with meat), Dohneiiong (pork curry), and Tungrymbai (fermented soybean).

Wangala Festival

A major Garo festival, also called the “100 Drums Festival,” celebrating harvest and music.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to April, when the weather is pleasant and waterfalls are in full flow.

How to Reach: By Air – Shillong Airport and Guwahati (Assam) nearby; By Road – Well-connected by NH40; By Train – Nearest railhead is Guwahati.

Local Transport: Taxis, shared cabs, and local buses are common in cities and towns.
