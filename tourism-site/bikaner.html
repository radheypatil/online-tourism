Bikaner – The Desert City of Rajasthan

Home to majestic forts, camel safaris, and the world-famous Bikaneri bhujia.

Introduction

Bikaner, located in the heart of the Thar Desert, is known for its vibrant culture, imposing forts, camel rides, and mouthwatering snacks. From the architectural marvel of Junagarh Fort to the holy Karni Mata Temple and the lively Camel Festival, Bikaner offers a unique Rajasthani experience.

Top Attractions

Junagarh Fort

A stunning fort built in the 16th century, known for its intricate carvings and palaces.

Karni Mata Temple

Famous for its thousands of holy rats, this temple is one of Rajasthan’s most unique sites.

Camel Festival

An annual celebration featuring camel races, dances, and colorful processions.

Local Experiences

Bikaneri Bhujia

Taste the world-famous spicy snack that originated in Bikaner.

Camel Safari

Ride camels through the golden dunes and enjoy Rajasthani folk performances.

Local Markets

Explore bustling bazaars offering handicrafts, jewelry, and textiles.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant desert weather.

How to Reach: By Air - Nal Airport (13 km from city); By Train - Bikaner Junction; By Road - Well-connected via highways.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, tongas, and taxis are popular in Bikaner.
