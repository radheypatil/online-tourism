Mizoram – The Land of the Highlanders

Rolling hills, vibrant tribal culture, bamboo forests, and warm hospitality await you.

Introduction

Mizoram, located in Northeast India, is known for its rolling hills, serene valleys, and rich tribal traditions. Often called the “Land of the Highlanders,” Mizoram offers untouched natural beauty, bamboo forests, and colorful festivals that make it a unique travel destination.

Top Attractions

Aizawl

The scenic capital, perched on hills, offering panoramic views and vibrant Mizo culture.

Champhai

Known as the “Rice Bowl of Mizoram,” it offers picturesque views of Myanmar’s hills.

Lunglei

A peaceful town surrounded by lush greenery, known for trekking and birdwatching.

Culture & Food

Cheraw Dance

The famous bamboo dance of Mizoram, performed with grace and rhythm during festivals.

Mizo Cuisine

Traditional dishes include Bai (mixed vegetables with pork), Bamboo shoot curry, and sticky rice.

Mizo Festivals

Chapchar Kut and Mim Kut are celebrated with music, dance, and feasts, showcasing Mizo traditions.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and cultural festivals.

How to Reach: By Air – Lengpui Airport (near Aizawl); By Train – Nearest major railhead is Silchar (Assam); By Road – Good connectivity with Assam and Tripura.

Local Transport: Shared taxis, local buses, and hired cabs are common for city and inter-town travel.
