Jaisalmer – The Golden City

Experience the golden charm of forts, havelis, and desert camel safaris.

Introduction

Jaisalmer, often called the "Golden City," stands majestically in the heart of the Thar Desert. Known for its yellow sandstone architecture, magnificent Jaisalmer Fort, and intricate havelis, the city is also the gateway to desert safaris and cultural performances. Its vibrant markets and starry desert nights make it a truly unforgettable destination.

Top Attractions

Jaisalmer Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this golden fort is a living fort with homes, shops, and temples inside.

Patwon Ki Haveli

An intricately carved haveli that reflects the grandeur of Jaisalmer's past.

Sam Sand Dunes

A must-visit for camel safaris, desert camping, and folk performances under the stars.

Local Experiences

Desert Festival

Held in February, this festival showcases camel races, folk music, and cultural performances.

Local Markets

Shop for handicrafts, embroidery, leather goods, and vibrant textiles.

Local Cuisine

Taste authentic Rajasthani food like Ker Sangri, Dal Baati Churma, and Ghotua Ladoo.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant desert weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Jaisalmer Airport; By Train - Jaisalmer Railway Station; By Road - Connected by NH11.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, jeeps, and camel rides.
