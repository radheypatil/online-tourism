Yoga by the Ganges

Rejuvenate your mind, body, and soul in Rishikesh – the Yoga Capital of the World.

Why Yoga in Rishikesh?

Rishikesh, nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas along the sacred Ganges River, is known as the world's yoga capital. Here, seekers from across the globe come to learn, practice, and experience the ancient art of yoga and meditation. Practicing yoga by the Ganges at sunrise or sunset, with the soothing sounds of flowing water and chants in the air, is a deeply spiritual and transformative experience.

The Experience

Morning Yoga by the River

Start your day with asanas and pranayama while the sun rises over the Himalayas.

Spiritual Evenings

End your day with meditation and the soulful Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat.

Ashram Life

Stay in traditional ashrams and learn yoga from experienced gurus.

Travel Tips

  • Best Time: October to March (pleasant weather).
  • Yoga Festivals: Attend the International Yoga Festival in March.
  • Stay: Ashrams, wellness retreats, and riverside resorts are popular choices.
  • What to Carry: Comfortable yoga clothing, mat, water bottle, and open mind.
