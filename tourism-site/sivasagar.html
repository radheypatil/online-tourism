Sivasagar, Assam

Where History Meets Culture

Famous Attractions

Rang Ghar

18th-century amphitheater of Ahom kings, famous for historical events and festivals.

Talatal Ghar

Underground palace of Ahom dynasty, featuring secret tunnels and rich architecture.

Sivasagar Sivadol

One of the tallest Shiva temples in India, surrounded by scenic ponds.

Kareng Ghar

Historical Ahom palace with impressive architecture and royal heritage.

Joysagar Tank

Large historical tank built by Ahom kings, offering scenic views and tranquility.

Gargaon Palace

Ancient Ahom palace showcasing traditional architecture and royal history.

Local Experience

Local Market

Explore bustling local markets, shop handicrafts, and experience Assamese village life.

Special Cuisine

Savor traditional Assamese dishes like Pitha, fish curry, and rice beer (Apong).

Local Experience

Immerse in Bihu dance and festivals, celebrating Assam's rich culture and traditions.
