Kovalam – Kerala’s Beach Paradise

Golden sands, lighthouse views, and thrilling water adventures by the Arabian Sea.

Introduction

Kovalam, located just 16 km from Thiruvananthapuram, is one of Kerala’s most celebrated beach destinations. Known for its crescent-shaped coastline, iconic lighthouse, and calm waters, it is the perfect place to relax or indulge in water sports like surfing and parasailing. Kovalam has long been a global tourist favorite, offering sun, sand, and serenity.

Top Attractions

Lighthouse Beach

The most popular beach in Kovalam, featuring a red-and-white striped lighthouse with panoramic views.

Hawah Beach

A quieter spot perfect for peaceful walks, sunbathing, and swimming.

Samudra Beach

Less crowded and ideal for solitude, evening walks, and fishing scenes.

Local Experiences

Water Sports

Try parasailing, surfing, and kayaking in the Arabian Sea.

Ayurvedic Massage

Rejuvenate with authentic Ayurvedic spa treatments offered at beachside resorts.

Kerala Cuisine

Savor seafood delicacies like Karimeen fry, prawn curry, and Sadya meals.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: September to March for pleasant beach weather.

How to Reach: By Air - Trivandrum International Airport (15 km away); By Train - Trivandrum Central; By Road - Well connected by taxis and buses.

Local Transport: Taxis, bikes for rent, and auto-rickshaws are common.
