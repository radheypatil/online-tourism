Manali – Adventure & Honeymoon Haven

A hub for adventure sports, snow activities, and romantic getaways.

Introduction

Manali, nestled in the Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh, is a dream destination for adventure seekers, honeymooners, and nature lovers. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks and pine forests, Manali offers everything from thrilling river rafting and paragliding to serene temple visits and cozy mountain stays. It is also a gateway to Leh–Ladakh and the famous Rohtang Pass.

Top Attractions

Hidimba Devi Temple

An ancient cave temple dedicated to Hidimba Devi, surrounded by cedar forests.

Rohtang Pass

A high mountain pass offering snow sports and breathtaking views of glaciers and peaks.

Solang Valley

The adventure capital of Manali with skiing, paragliding, and zorbing activities.

Local Experiences

Paragliding

Soar over the valleys of Manali and enjoy an adrenaline-filled sky adventure.

River Rafting

Experience thrilling rafting on the Beas River with stunning mountain backdrops.

Vashisht Hot Springs

Relax in natural hot water springs believed to have healing properties.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March to June for adventure activities; December to February for snow and honeymoon trips.

How to Reach: By Air - Bhuntar Airport (50 km); By Road - Well-connected to Delhi, Chandigarh, and Shimla; By Train - Joginder Nagar (closest narrow-gauge railway).

Local Transport: Taxis, buses, and bike rentals are popular for exploring nearby attractions.
