Lakshadweep – Tropical Paradise

Turquoise waters, coral reefs, and pristine beaches make Lakshadweep the ultimate island escape.

Introduction

Lakshadweep, India’s smallest Union Territory, is a group of 36 breathtaking islands scattered in the Arabian Sea. Known for its coral reefs, lagoons, and white sandy beaches, it offers the perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and exotic island culture. It’s a haven for divers, honeymooners, and nature lovers.

Top Attractions

Agatti

Gateway to Lakshadweep with crystal-clear lagoons, ideal for snorkeling and diving.

Bangaram

A stunning uninhabited island famous for coral reefs and turquoise waters.

Kavaratti

The capital island known for its lagoon, mosques, and marine aquarium.

Minicoy

Unique culture, traditional dances, and one of the oldest lighthouses in Lakshadweep.

Local Experiences

Scuba Diving

Explore the vibrant marine life and coral reefs of the Arabian Sea.

Island Hopping

Visit multiple islands on boat cruises and soak in tropical bliss.

Local Culture

Enjoy coconut-based cuisine, folk dances, and traditions of island life.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant tropical weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Flights from Kochi to Agatti Airport. By Sea – Passenger ships from Kochi to Kavaratti, Minicoy, and others.

Local Transport: Boats, ferries, and bicycles for moving around islands.
