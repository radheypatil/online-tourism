Shimla – The Queen of Hills

Colonial charm, Mall Road strolls, and panoramic Himalayan views.

Introduction

Shimla, the capital of Himachal Pradesh, is a hill station steeped in colonial heritage and natural beauty. Nicknamed the "Queen of Hills," it is known for its pleasant climate, snow-capped peaks, and charming architecture from the British era. Visitors flock to Shimla for leisurely walks along the Mall Road, breathtaking views from the Ridge, and the iconic toy train ride through the Himalayas.

Top Attractions

Mall Road

The heart of Shimla, lined with shops, cafes, and colonial buildings.

Christ Church

A Neo-Gothic landmark on the Ridge, one of the oldest churches in North India.

Jakhu Temple

Dedicated to Lord Hanuman, located atop Jakhu Hill with sweeping views.

Local Experiences

Toy Train Ride

Enjoy the UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla Railway with stunning Himalayan views.

Skiing in Kufri

Experience snow adventures at Kufri, just 16 km from Shimla.

Lakkar Bazaar Shopping

Famous for wooden handicrafts, souvenirs, and Himachali specialties.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March to June for pleasant weather; December to February for snow lovers.

How to Reach: By Air - Jubbarhatti Airport (23 km); By Train - Kalka–Shimla Railway; By Road - Well-connected by buses and taxis from Delhi and Chandigarh.

Local Transport: Taxis, auto-rickshaws, and walking are common ways to explore the compact city.
