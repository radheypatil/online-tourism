Haryana – The Land of History and Progress

From Kurukshetra’s epics to Gurugram’s skyscrapers – experience tradition and modernity together.

Introduction

Haryana, located in northern India, is a state where ancient history meets modern development. Known for its role in the epic Mahabharata at Kurukshetra and its modern IT hub Gurugram, Haryana offers spiritual heritage, Mughal gardens, cultural fairs, and growing urban landscapes.

Top Attractions

Gurugram

A bustling IT and corporate hub with malls, nightlife, and modern lifestyle.

Kurukshetra

The land of the Mahabharata, known for Brahma Sarovar and spiritual sites.

Pinjore Gardens

A 17th-century Mughal garden with fountains, pavilions, and heritage beauty.

Faridabad

Known for Surajkund Mela, crafts, and cultural heritage.

Culture & Food

Surajkund Mela

A world-famous crafts fair celebrating folk art, music, and traditions.

Traditional Food

Taste kadhi-chawal, bajra roti, lassi, and makki di roti with sarson da saag.

Folk Music & Dance

Enjoy energetic folk dances and soulful music that reflect Haryana’s spirit.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air – Delhi & Chandigarh Airports nearby; By Train – Kurukshetra, Gurugram well-connected; By Road – Excellent highway connectivity.

Local Transport: Taxis, buses, and auto-rickshaws are widely available.
