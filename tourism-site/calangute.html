Calangute – The Queen of Beaches

Famous for water sports, nightlife, and a lively atmosphere.

Introduction

Calangute, located in North Goa, is popularly known as the "Queen of Beaches." Bustling with tourists year-round, it’s the perfect destination for adventure, relaxation, and nightlife. From thrilling water sports to vibrant shacks and clubs, Calangute promises an unforgettable Goan experience.

Top Attractions

Calangute Beach

The main attraction with golden sands, waves, and a buzzing vibe.

Baga Beach

Famous for clubs like Tito’s and Mambo’s, just next to Calangute.

Beach Shacks

Chill at lively shacks with seafood, cocktails, and music by the sea.

Local Experiences

Water Sports

Try parasailing, jet skiing, and banana boat rides on the waves.

Nightlife

Experience vibrant nightlife with beach clubs and live music.

Shopping

Shop for clothes, jewelry, and souvenirs at Calangute Market.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to March for beach fun and water sports.

How to Reach: By Air - Dabolim Airport (40 km); By Train - Thivim Station (19 km); By Road - Well-connected by buses and cabs.

Local Transport: Bike rentals, taxis, and auto-rickshaws are easily available.
