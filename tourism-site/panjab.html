Punjab – The Land of Five Rivers

Experience spirituality, heritage, hospitality, and flavors in the vibrant heart of India.

Introduction

Punjab, the land of five rivers, is renowned for its rich culture, spiritual heritage, colorful festivals, and warm hospitality. From the Golden Temple’s divine serenity to the architectural elegance of Chandigarh, Punjab is a perfect blend of tradition and modernity. It is also the land of bhangra, giddha, and lip-smacking Punjabi food.

Top Attractions

Amritsar

Home to the Golden Temple, Wagah Border ceremony, and a hub of Punjabi culture and cuisine.

Chandigarh

India’s first planned city, known for Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, and modern architecture by Le Corbusier.

Ludhiana

The Manchester of India, famous for textile industry, culture, and agricultural heritage.

Patiala

Known for Qila Mubarak, royal heritage, and the famous ‘Patiala Peg’ tradition.

Anandpur Sahib

A sacred Sikh city, home to Takht Sri Kesgarh Sahib and the grand Hola Mohalla festival.

Culture & Food

Bhangra & Giddha

Vibrant folk dances that showcase Punjab’s joyous spirit and cultural pride.

Punjabi Cuisine

From sarson da saag with makki di roti to butter chicken and lassi, Punjab’s food is globally loved.

Festivals

Lohri, Baisakhi, and Hola Mohalla are celebrated with unmatched energy and joy.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air – Amritsar & Chandigarh Airports; By Train – Major cities well connected; By Road – Excellent highway network.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cycle rickshaws, cabs, and buses are available.
