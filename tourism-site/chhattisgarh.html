Chhattisgarh – The Tribal Heart of India

Discover breathtaking waterfalls, ancient temples, and vibrant tribal traditions.

Introduction

Chhattisgarh, located in central India, is known for its lush forests, magnificent waterfalls, rich tribal culture, and archaeological treasures. From the modern capital Raipur to the cultural heart of Bastar, the state offers an immersive journey into India’s natural and cultural heritage.

Top Attractions

Raipur

The bustling capital city with lakes, temples, and modern shopping hubs.

Jagdalpur (Bastar)

Known for its tribal culture and the famous Bastar Dussehra festival.

Chitrakote

Often called the ‘Niagara Falls of India’, a majestic horseshoe-shaped waterfall.

Sirpur

An archaeological site with ancient temples, monasteries, and historical ruins.

Culture & Food

Bastar Dussehra

A unique 75-day festival showcasing tribal rituals, culture, and traditions.

Chhattisgarhi Cuisine

Savor unique delicacies like Chila, Faraa, and Bafauri, reflecting rural flavors.

Handicrafts

Tribal artisans create beautiful bell-metal, bamboo, and terracotta crafts.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Raipur Airport; By Train – Raipur & Jagdalpur Junctions; By Road – National highways connect major cities.

Local Transport: Buses, auto-rickshaws, and taxis are common modes of travel.
