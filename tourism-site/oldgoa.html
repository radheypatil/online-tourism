Old Goa – Historic & Spiritual Heritage

Home to majestic churches and UNESCO World Heritage monuments.

Introduction

Old Goa, once the capital of Portuguese India, is renowned for its grand churches, convents, and spiritual history. The city is home to several UNESCO World Heritage Sites such as the Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral, and Church of St. Francis of Assisi. With its rich blend of Indo-Portuguese architecture and serene riverside views, Old Goa is a must-visit destination for history enthusiasts and spiritual seekers alike.

Top Attractions

Basilica of Bom Jesus

Houses the mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Se Cathedral

One of the largest churches in Asia, famous for its Portuguese-Gothic architecture.

Church of St. Francis of Assisi

A beautiful blend of Gothic and Manueline styles with intricate interiors.

Local Experiences

Heritage Walk

Explore the history and architecture of Old Goa on guided walking tours.

Mandovi River Cruise

Enjoy scenic views of the historic city from the Mandovi River.

Local Markets

Shop for souvenirs, handicrafts, and Goan specialties in Old Goa’s vibrant markets.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: November to February for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

How to Reach: By Air - Dabolim Airport (28 km); By Train - Margao Station (30 km); By Road - Well-connected by buses, taxis, and rental vehicles.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and rental scooters are convenient for exploring Old Goa.
