Jodhpur – The Blue City

Explore Mehrangarh Fort, blue-painted streets, and the vibrant Marwar culture.

Introduction

Jodhpur, the second-largest city of Rajasthan, is affectionately known as the "Blue City" due to its vivid blue-painted houses around the Mehrangarh Fort. A symbol of Rajput valor and tradition, Jodhpur is a captivating mix of heritage forts, lively bazaars, and desert landscapes. Its culture, festivals, and food make it an unforgettable experience for travelers.

Top Attractions

Mehrangarh Fort

One of India’s largest forts, offering panoramic views of the Blue City.

Jaswant Thada

A beautiful marble cenotaph built in memory of Maharaja Jaswant Singh II.

Umaid Bhawan Palace

A grand palace that is part royal residence, part luxury hotel, and part museum.

Local Experiences

Clock Tower Market

Shop for spices, handicrafts, and antiques in the bustling Sardar Market.

Makhaniya Lassi

Taste Jodhpur’s famous creamy lassi, a refreshing local delicacy.

Marwar Festival

A celebration of Rajasthani folk music, dance, and traditions.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for cool desert weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Jodhpur Airport; By Train - Jodhpur Junction; By Road - Connected via NH62 and NH114.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cabs, and heritage walks.
