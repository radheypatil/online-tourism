Uttarakhand – Devbhoomi, Land of Gods

Experience the serene Himalayas, sacred rivers, thrilling treks, and divine spirituality.

Introduction

Uttarakhand, fondly called Devbhoomi, is a land of temples, mountains, valleys, and rivers. With sacred cities like Haridwar and Rishikesh, charming hill stations like Mussoorie and Nainital, wildlife at Jim Corbett, and the breathtaking Valley of Flowers, Uttarakhand offers spirituality, adventure, and natural beauty in abundance.

Top Attractions

Dehradun

Capital city surrounded by Doon Valley, known for its scenic views, educational institutions, and nearby hill stations.

Haridwar

A sacred city on the banks of Ganga, famous for Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri and Kumbh Mela.

Rishikesh

Yoga capital of the world, popular for meditation, rafting, and iconic suspension bridges.

Mussoorie

The ‘Queen of Hills’, famous for its colonial charm, waterfalls, and Himalayan views.

Nainital

A lake district surrounded by lush hills, offering boating, trekking, and scenic beauty.

Jim Corbett (Ramnagar)

India’s first national park, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger and diverse wildlife.

Auli

A top skiing destination in India, with snow-covered peaks and adventure sports.

Valley of Flowers

A UNESCO World Heritage site, known for its seasonal blooms and Himalayan landscapes.

Local Experiences

Ganga Aarti

A divine experience at Haridwar and Rishikesh, with chants, lamps, and devotion on the Ganga ghats.

Kumaoni & Garhwali Cuisine

Relish local dishes like Aloo Ke Gutke, Bhatt ki Churdkani, and Mandua roti.

Adventure Sports

Enjoy river rafting, trekking, skiing, and camping amidst the Himalayan landscapes.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March to June (summer) and September to November (autumn). Auli for winters (skiing).

How to Reach: By Air – Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun); By Train – Dehradun, Haridwar, Kathgodam; By Road – well connected by highways and buses.

Local Transport: Taxis, buses, and shared jeeps are common for hilly routes.
