Chittorgarh – The Pride of Rajasthan

Home to the grand Chittorgarh Fort, echoing stories of valor and sacrifice.

Introduction

Chittorgarh, located in southern Rajasthan, is renowned for its majestic Chittorgarh Fort, one of the largest forts in India. The city embodies Rajput pride, heroism, and the legendary tales of Queen Padmini and Maharana Pratap. Its forts, palaces, and temples make it a living museum of Rajasthan’s glorious past.

Top Attractions

Chittorgarh Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this fort is a sprawling complex of palaces, gates, and towers.

Vijay Stambha

The Tower of Victory, built by Rana Kumbha to commemorate his triumph over the Sultan of Malwa.

Padmini Palace

Associated with Rani Padmini, this palace stands as a symbol of beauty and sacrifice.

Local Experiences

Kirti Stambha

A 12th-century tower dedicated to Lord Adinath, showcasing exquisite Jain architecture.

Meera Temple

Dedicated to the poet-saint Meera Bai, a symbol of devotion and spirituality.

Local Markets

Bustling bazaars offering handicrafts, Rajasthani jewelry, and traditional fabrics.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

How to Reach: By Air - Udaipur Airport (115 km); By Train - Chittorgarh Railway Station; By Road - Well-connected by highways and buses.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and local buses are available for commuting.
