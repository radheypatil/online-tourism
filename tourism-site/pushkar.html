Pushkar – The Holy City of Rajasthan

Famous for Pushkar Lake, Brahma Temple, and the vibrant Camel Fair.

Introduction

Pushkar is one of the oldest cities in India, known for its sacred Pushkar Lake, the rare Brahma Temple, and its colorful bazaars. Every year, thousands flock here for the world-famous Pushkar Camel Fair, blending spirituality with culture. Its serene ghats and vibrant atmosphere make it a must-visit destination in Rajasthan.

Top Attractions

Pushkar Lake

A sacred lake surrounded by 52 ghats, where pilgrims take holy dips.

Brahma Temple

One of the very few temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma.

Pushkar Camel Fair

A grand cultural event featuring camel trading, folk performances, and festivities.

Local Experiences

Pushkar Bazaars

Colorful markets offering handicrafts, jewelry, textiles, and souvenirs.

Pushkar Cafes

Chill at rooftop cafes with views of the lake and temples.

Local Festivals

Witness vibrant cultural festivals, music, and dance performances.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March; especially during the Camel Fair in November.

How to Reach: By Air - Jaipur Airport (150 km); By Train - Ajmer Junction (11 km); By Road - Buses and taxis connect Pushkar with major cities.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cycles, and walking are popular ways to explore.
