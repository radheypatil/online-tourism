Karnataka – Heritage, Hills & Technology

From modern Bengaluru to ancient Hampi, Karnataka is a land of contrasts – technology, temples, beaches, and coffee plantations.

Introduction

Karnataka, located in southern India, is a state where heritage meets modernity. The IT hub Bengaluru, the royal palaces of Mysuru, the ruins of Hampi, and the scenic beauty of Coorg make it a diverse travel destination. From beaches in Gokarna to ancient temples in Badami, Karnataka offers something for every traveler.

Top Attractions

Bengaluru

India's Silicon Valley, known for gardens, nightlife, and technology hubs.

Mysuru

The City of Palaces, famous for Mysore Palace and Dasara festival.

Hampi

UNESCO World Heritage Site with ancient ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Coorg

A hill station famous for coffee plantations, trekking, and waterfalls.

Gokarna

Known for its pristine beaches and spiritual temples.

Udupi

Famous for Udupi Krishna Temple and its unique cuisine.

Mangalore

A coastal city with temples, beaches, and spicy Mangalorean cuisine.

Badami

Famous for rock-cut cave temples and ancient architecture.

Culture & Food

Mysore Dasara

A grand festival showcasing Karnataka’s royal heritage and traditions.

Masala Dosa

A world-famous South Indian dish that originated in Karnataka.

Yakshagana

A traditional theater art form with colorful costumes and storytelling.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Kempegowda International Airport (Bengaluru); By Train – Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangalore; By Road – Excellent connectivity across South India.

Local Transport: Metro (Bengaluru), buses, cabs, and auto-rickshaws are widely available.
