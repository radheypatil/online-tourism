Jaipur – The Pink City

Discover the royal forts, palaces, and vibrant bazaars of Rajasthan’s capital.

Introduction

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is lovingly called the "Pink City" for its rosy-hued architecture. Known for its royal heritage, majestic forts, and bustling bazaars, Jaipur is a unique blend of history and modern charm. From the iconic Hawa Mahal to the grand Amer Fort, Jaipur offers an unforgettable cultural experience.

Top Attractions

Hawa Mahal

A stunning palace with intricate latticework, known as the "Palace of Winds".

Amer Fort

A majestic fort with a blend of Hindu and Mughal architecture, overlooking Maota Lake.

City Palace

The royal residence showcasing Jaipur’s regal art and culture.

Local Experiences

Shopping at Johari Bazaar

A paradise for jewelry, textiles, handicrafts, and traditional Rajasthani souvenirs.

Cultural Festivals

Experience Jaipur’s vibrant festivals like Teej and the Elephant Festival.

Rajasthani Cuisine

Relish authentic dishes like Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, and Laal Maas.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather and festivals.

How to Reach: By Air - Jaipur International Airport; By Train - Jaipur Junction; By Road - Connected via NH48 and NH52.

Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, cabs, and local buses are widely available.
