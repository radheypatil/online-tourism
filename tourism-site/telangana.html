Telangana – Heritage & Modernity

From the bustling streets of Hyderabad to the ancient forts of Warangal, Telangana blends culture, history, and innovation.

Introduction

Telangana, India’s youngest state, is a mix of old-world heritage and modern development. The capital Hyderabad is known for its IT hub, biryani, and Charminar, while cities like Warangal and Nizamabad reflect the state’s historical grandeur. With temples, forts, lakes, and cuisine, Telangana is a must-visit destination.

Top Attractions

Hyderabad

Home to Charminar, Golconda Fort, Ramoji Film City, and the famous Hyderabadi biryani.

Warangal

Known for its historical Warangal Fort, Thousand Pillar Temple, and Kakatiya heritage sites.

Nizamabad

A city with a blend of history and modernity, famous for its fort and temples.

Khammam

Famous for the Khammam Fort and serene nature spots like Lakaram Lake.

Culture & Food

Festivals

Bathukamma, a floral festival, is unique to Telangana and showcases its cultural vibrance.

Cuisine

Hyderabadi Biryani, Haleem, and Telangana’s spicy curries are world famous.

Heritage

Telangana’s forts, palaces, and Islamic architecture reflect its glorious past.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: October to March for pleasant weather.

How to Reach: By Air – Hyderabad International Airport; By Train – Major stations in Hyderabad, Warangal, Nizamabad; By Road – Well-connected highways.

Local Transport: Metro, buses, auto-rickshaws, and cabs in Hyderabad; local transport in other cities.
