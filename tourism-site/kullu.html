Kullu – Valley of Gods

Known for Dussehra festival, lush valleys, and adventure sports in the Himalayas.

Introduction

Kullu, located in Himachal Pradesh, is often called the “Valley of Gods” for its scenic beauty and rich cultural traditions. Surrounded by snow-clad mountains, apple orchards, and pine forests, Kullu is a paradise for nature lovers and thrill-seekers. The town is especially famous for its grand Dussehra festival, where people from across the world gather to witness the vibrant cultural celebrations.

Top Attractions

Manikaran

A famous pilgrimage site with hot springs, Gurudwara, and temples in Parvati Valley.

Great Himalayan National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage site, rich in flora, fauna, and trekking routes.

Kullu Dussehra

The world-famous week-long festival celebrating Lord Raghunath with processions and fairs.

Local Experiences

River Rafting

Thrilling rafting adventures in the Beas River, perfect for adrenaline seekers.

Paragliding

Fly over the beautiful valleys and enjoy breathtaking aerial views of Kullu-Manali.

Trekking

Explore scenic trails through pine forests, meadows, and snow-capped peaks.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit: March to June for pleasant weather; September to October for Dussehra festival; December to February for snow activities.

How to Reach: By Air – Kullu-Manali Airport in Bhuntar (10 km); By Train – Joginder Nagar Railway Station (95 km); By Road – Well connected by buses and taxis from Delhi, Chandigarh, and Shimla.

Local Transport: Taxis, rented bikes, and buses are the best ways to explore Kullu and nearby valleys.
